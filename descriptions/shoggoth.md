# Description of the Wildcat Shoggoth

A vast eldritch manifestation of the Wildcat mascot fills a cavernous, cathedral-dark space. The creature has no single body: it is an enormous interconnected mass of glossy black flesh, serpentine tendrils, coiling appendages and partially formed limbs, spreading outward across almost the entire frame.

The Wildcat mascot repeatedly emerges from this mass at different scales and degrees of completion. Angular feline heads protrude from tentacles and folds of flesh; some are attached to rudimentary torsos or skeletal humanoid arms, while others appear almost organically grown from the creature. The largest and most complete Wildcat face occupies the visual centre, elevated above the surrounding mass like the creature's temporary primary identity.

The mascot heads retain a sharply geometric, almost papercraft-like anatomy: extremely tall triangular ears, a broad faceted forehead, angular cheek planes, a pointed lower muzzle and chin, heavy dark eyebrows, and rectangular golden-yellow eyes. Their pale stone-grey faces contrast strongly against the black organism.

Between them, enormous amber feline eyes open directly from the creature's body. These range from small embedded eyes to huge luminous organs with vertical black pupils. Circular lamprey-like mouths lined with teeth appear among the folds. Long, pale, skeletal hands and claws emerge at irregular intervals, giving parts of the mass a disturbingly humanoid anatomy.

The creature is not visually random. Its tendrils repeatedly curl into deliberate, almost ornamental shapes, while recurring Wildcat faces suggest that the entire organism is continuously attempting to instantiate the same identity in different places.

The palette is almost monochromatic: oily black, charcoal and desaturated bone-grey, punctuated by warm amber-gold eyes. Surfaces are highly detailed and physically rendered, with wet black reflections, fine scratches and weathering on the pale faces, and subtle specular highlights defining the otherwise nearly black anatomy.

The composition is symmetrical enough to feel monumental without becoming formally symmetrical. The central Wildcat face provides a strong focal point, surrounded by increasingly smaller manifestations toward the edges. The camera is positioned approximately at the creature's level, with a very slight low-angle quality that makes the central figure imposing.

The setting resembles a vast gothic industrial cathedral or subterranean hall. Almost all environmental detail disappears into darkness. Narrow shafts of cold light descend from high behind the subject, creating strong rim lighting around ears, tendrils and claws. The foreground is a shallow black reflective floor covered by the creature's spreading mass. The overall presentation is cinematic, theatrical and extremely dark, with the subject emerging primarily through silhouette, reflected highlights and glowing eyes.
