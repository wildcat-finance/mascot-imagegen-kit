# Normal Wildcat mascot standing alone in the same visual language

A single Wildcat mascot stands alone at the centre of a vast, dark, cathedral-like stage, occupying the same position and approximate scale as the principal figure in the Shoggoth composition.

The character is completely normal and anatomically coherent: one head, one torso, two arms and two legs, with no tentacles, additional eyes, mouths, duplicated faces, mutations or surrounding organic matter.

The Wildcat is an angular anthropomorphic feline whose design is constructed from pronounced geometric planes. The head has extremely tall, narrow triangular ears; a broad wedge-shaped upper skull; sharply faceted cheeks; a tapered muzzle; and a small pointed chin. The planes of the face should remain visibly structural rather than being softened into ordinary feline anatomy.

Its eyes are distinctive rectangular golden-yellow feline eyes with dark vertical pupils, sitting beneath strong dark angular eyebrows. The expression is neutral and composed: eyes open normally, brows relaxed, mouth closed, neither smiling nor threatening. The character looks directly toward the camera.

The body should follow the same angular design language as the head: lean adult humanoid proportions, clearly articulated shoulders, arms and legs, with restrained geometric simplification rather than exaggerated musculature. The posture is neutral and balanced, standing upright with the feet naturally separated and the arms resting loosely at the sides. Nothing about the pose communicates aggression, heroism, comedy or submission. It should read as the canonical "default state" of the character.

The mascot stands on a nearly black, subtly reflective floor. No other characters or objects occupy the stage.

Behind it is the same immense, barely visible gothic-industrial interior as the Shoggoth image: towering vertical architecture disappearing into darkness, with only fragments of arches, columns or structural ribs visible.

Lighting comes primarily from high behind and above the character. Narrow, cold shafts of backlight descend through the darkness, producing a strong silhouette and thin rim highlights around the enormous ears, shoulders and sides of the body. A restrained frontal fill reveals the face and body without destroying the darkness of the scene. The yellow eyes provide the only significant warm colour accent.

The camera remains almost straight-on at approximately torso or chest height, with a subtle low-angle perspective. Use a wide cinematic frame with substantial negative space around the solitary figure. Keep the character exactly centred.

The rendering should have the same high-detail, physically grounded quality as the Shoggoth image: dark cinematic realism, finely textured surfaces, subtle wear and material variation, deep blacks, restrained highlights and strong chiaroscuro.

The result should feel less like a character portrait and more like a neutral reference specimen presented on the Shoggoth's stage: the canonical Wildcat isolated from all narrative context, ready to serve as the common base from which individually named ecosystem characters can subsequently diverge.
